    Tribute to the Police

    Police

    The Police remain one of the greatest rock bands of all time, with a fan base that’s varied from young to old.

    Check availability Their current page

    About Police

    Message in a Bottle recreates the magic and original flavour of Sting, Andy Summers and Stewart Copeland of yesteryear, while producing an energetic and fresh live show of today leaving the audience wanting more whenever they hit the stage.

    This four piece plays all of The Police’s greatest hits which are some of the most popular songs ever created including “Roxanne,” “Every Breath You Take” and “Message in a Bottle”. They continue to play many high profile shows such as Vail Hot Summer Nights, Orchard Summer Concerts, Southlands Mall Concert Series, Elway’s Backyard Summer Concerts and countless private events. If you’re a Police fan, this band is a must see, and if you’re not, you will be after the show.
